Why Hamburg

  • Airbus teams work across sites every day. Seeing the company from another site would give me a wider view than Toulouse alone.
  • Working every day with German colleagues, in English, would make me better at international teamwork.

How it fits with my employer

Airbus is a European company with sites in France, Germany, Spain and the UK. A mobility inside the group keeps me in the same tools and the same culture, while adding a second site and a wider network to my profile.
